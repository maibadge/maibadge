These are the steps on how to add your images into your keychain!

----- JPEG FILES -----
[1] Open Microsoft Paint and create a new image of 240 x 240 pixels
[2] Save it on your computer as a ".jpg" file
[3] Drag and drop the file into the "image" folder

----- GIF FILES -----
[1] You may use EzGif to convert a video to Gif format. https://ezgif.com/video-to-gif
[2] The keychain has a limited storage of 1 Megabyte. You may crop and compress your Gif to a small size.
[3] Next, use EzGif and choose 'Resize' to 240 x 240 pixels. https://ezgif.com/maker
[2] Save it on your computer as a ".gif" file
[3] Drag and drop the file into the "image" folder
